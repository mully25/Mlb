Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

MLB Stats Graphics is a personal project that generates and publishes MLB statistical leaderboard graphics. This site does not collect, store, or share any personal information from visitors.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. This website displays static images only. No cookies, tracking scripts, or analytics are used.

Third-Party Data

Statistical data displayed in the graphics is sourced from publicly available MLB data (MLB.com, Baseball Reference, Baseball Savant). No personal user data is involved.

TikTok Integration

This app connects to TikTok solely to publish graphics to a single creator account. No viewer or follower data is accessed or stored.

Contact
